# Platforms, measurement qualification and economics

## Reuse before procurement

Prefer a deployment's existing presentation/business stack, candidate SEO contracts, field writers and official search diagnostics. Inspect accounts before creating properties: owner, tenant/site/property, environment, identity/scopes, existing contract/invoice, renewal, current capacity and deployment relation. Uninspected is unknown, not absent.

Preserve the actual boundaries between website implementation, experience, marketing connectors, data-purpose decisions, commercial decisions and competent subject/locale review. Reconcile current roles rather than treating an old plan as a live routing database. A skill supplies methodology; it does not create another publisher or ledger.

## Platform decision packet

For each new paid tool, cloud service or runtime, state:

1. Concrete question or missing capability.
2. Existing alternatives and why they are insufficient.
3. New useful data/function, export/API requirements and applicable scope.
4. Limits, terms, region, security/data exposure and integration burden.
5. Full cost including existing costs, incremental units, human review, maintenance, contingency and tax.
6. A bounded pilot, success/exit criterion, owner and reversible fallback.

Optional backlink/keyword tooling must answer actual questions and expose the data an agent needs. A low-cost tier without required export/API access may not suffice. Verify current prices and quotas before recommending a purchase. Do not provision multiple clouds, paid agent runtimes, retrieval infrastructure or a second analytics pipeline without a demonstrated need.

An optional reporting dashboard presents qualified aggregates; it is neither an authority nor a customer database. A warehouse requires demonstrated volume, retention and integration needs, qualified region/access, estimated query costs and deletion controls. Neither is a prerequisite for an offline pilot.

Assisted execution may suffice. A dedicated runtime needs useful recurring work, real connectors, operational responsibility and favorable comparative cost. Subscription access and API spend are different budget categories. Follow the operator's current model/router policy; choices in a historical proposal are not universal rules or permission to create another permanent provider runtime.

## Analytics, tag management and consent

Inventory native emitters, tags, containers, analytics/consent tooling, server-side forwarding, replay and third-party scripts before adding instrumentation. Define one emitter per logical event and explicit deduplication. A pure event builder is not evidence of transmission.

Qualify purpose, data fields, surfaces, destinations, automatic collection, retention and consent behavior. Inspect URL/path/query/title, sensitive category, referrer, identifiers and free text as well as named event parameters. Supplier definitions do not exhaust an operator's privacy requirements; use the competent data owner.

Keep calendars, login, forms, checkout and member/private areas outside marketing/replay unless a reviewed deployment design permits a specific use. Aggregate operational reporting can remain separate. Do not silently disable functional booking or consent as part of tagging.

Test accept/refuse/revoke with synthetic scenarios and actual network destinations. A proposed consent configuration is not a universal legal conclusion. Server-side tagging does not by itself establish an eligible purpose or adequate minimization. Aggregation/suppression thresholds belong to the responsible data authority. Do not join person-level sensitive and marketing sources for SEO convenience.

## Economics

C0 denotes existing cost not yet inventoried; unknown C0 is not zero. Separate setup, recurring incremental software, human/subject/linguistic review, contingency, current contracts and taxes. Internal time consumes capacity.

Calculate a deployment-specific budget from actual scope, current tariffs and qualified human capacity. A planning figure is neither a current quote nor approved expenditure. No platform is purchased by using this skill.

Assess return using approved contribution margin, actual capacity, cancellations/refunds and attribution limits. Revenue or service price is not margin. For a scenario, break-even additional conversions equal incremental recurring cost divided by validated contribution per conversion; initial investment has separate payback. If margin is unknown, label scenarios explicitly.

Separate selling advertising inventory from purchasing acquisition ads. Any paid acquisition trial is outside a base organic plan unless authorized. Verify current sector, targeting and platform policies. Consent does not override prohibited-use policy. Do not introduce sensitive-interest audiences, customer lists or remarketing through an SEO task without the necessary purpose and mandate.

## Integrate new planning material

Use documents for their actual contribution and dates. Preserve later stronger observations over historical audits and correct stale claims against primary sources. Budgets and platform choices remain proposals until adopted. A document does not authorize buying tools, activating a runtime or changing a live site.
