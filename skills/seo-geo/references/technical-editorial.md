# Technical and editorial checks

## Intent and content

Map intent × service/product × page × locale × funnel stage; distinguish brand/navigation/information/comparison/transaction. Use real queries when available. Record geography, period, supplier and gaps for volume/difficulty estimates. Do not create pages for accents, synonyms or fictitious offices. Compare query→URL evidence before diagnosing harmful cannibalization.

Write specific title, description, H1 and answers. Character counts are heuristics, not Google eligibility rules; Google can rewrite titles/snippets. Wix focus keyword is an editing aid. Google ignores meta keywords; absence is not a ranking incident.

Provide purpose, audience, scope/limitations, inclusions, preparation, price/conditions, real author/professional/reviewer, sources and next action. In health verify current official sources and competent review. Demand does not authorize false specialty or guaranteed fitness/external approval. Material/review dates must be real.

Price in title is an optional maintained-source experiment. Align card/body/metadata/schema/checkout and keep a no-price fallback. Do not put price in slugs. A separate SERP price line does not prove price belonged to title or a specific schema.

## Technical eligibility

Capture initial/final status/redirects and actual headers/head; test HTTPS/host, 404/410/503, soft 404 and intermittent failures. Essential text/links should be present in initial HTML. Cleaned markdown cannot establish missing head tags/headers. Distinguish SSR and hydrated DOM.

Separate crawl permission, robots meta/header, eligibility, actual index, engine-selected canonical, impressions and conversion. Robots/noindex is not access control. Crawl blocking can prevent reading noindex. Preserve generic vs bot-specific directives and applicable restrictive rules.

Audit single absolute appropriate canonical, duplicates, locale, snippets and social metadata. Declared canonical is not engine selection. Preserve intentional private/preview/error/filter/internal-search policy.

Identify existing sitemap/robots publisher. Validate index/children, host, final canonical eligible 200 URLs, language links and real material lastmod. No competing root routes. Compare registry with published renderer; registration alone does not establish live correctness.

Check links, orphan/deep pages using all inventories, parameter explosions, confusing anchors and broken destinations. A scrape error is not proof of 404; missing map entry is not deindexation.

Preserve valuable legacy URLs and semantics. Test locale, encoding, query policy, loops, responsible layers and rollback. For Wix read wix.md: generic redirect-chain assumptions are unsafe.

## Internationalization/accessibility

Website language, consultation/support language and service jurisdiction are separate. Menu/fallback is not full translation. Use appropriate lang/direction, self-canonical and reciprocal hreflang among complete available accepted equivalents. Define x-default from a real entry policy. Test root without preference, explicit selection and SSR/JS differences; preserve user choice.

Check keyboard/focus, accessible names/states, reader navigation, labels/errors, contrast, mobile, zoom/reflow and RTL. Automated checks cover part of accessibility; do not certify an entire site from a score.

## Media and documents

Inventory asset→page/locale, rights/creator/license, alt/caption, dimensions and format. Use supported HTML image sources and context; do not rely on CSS-only backgrounds for image discovery. Alt describes function/content, not keyword lists. Do not hide essential medical text in images.

Optimize formats/responsive variants, dimensions and only genuinely critical priority. Remove sensitive metadata; no patient imagery. Do not claim EXIF/AI imagery guarantees ranking. For useful video add accessible player, captions/transcript, context, thumbnail and truthful metadata, not a daily video quota.

Public documents must be intentional, current and accessible. Certificates, completed consents, invoices and patient files are not acquisition assets.

## Entities/schema

Map real organization/site/page/service/professional/article/image/breadcrumb identities with stable IDs and node owner. Compare native Wix graph before adding another. Resolve conflicting IDs/prices/entities at the source; preserve unrelated scripts/consent.

Organization, WebPage, Service, Offer, Person, Article, ImageObject and BreadcrumbList are conditional candidates. Medical review properties reflect actual review. No fake clinic, staff, specialty, ratings or Product reclassification to seek rich results.

Separate JSON syntax, visible/factual semantic accuracy and currently supported feature eligibility. Valid Service/Offer does not guarantee price display. Safe script serialization and literal tag text inside scripts/styles/comments need behavior tests.

Dated source snapshot (2026-11-02): Google FAQ rich results ceased appearing 2026-05-07. Keep useful FAQs; do not condition a promise on the old health/government exception. Reverify mutable sources before a dependent recommendation.

## Experience and external authority

Use field LCP/INP/CLS p75 by device when available: snapshot thresholds 2.5s/200ms/0.1. Missing sample is unknown. Lighthouse without interaction does not measure INP; TBT is not INP. Record lab conditions. Scores do not guarantee rankings.

Improve assets/scripts/hydration/fonts/cache from measured cause. Never cache private identity/clinical content publicly or remove consent/auth for scores. Price cache needs explicit freshness independent of editorial qualification.

Check listing eligibility/ownership and coherent identity before edits. Online-only businesses are excluded from Google Business Profile in the dated source; an administrative address does not prove in-person contact. Do not invent/delete profiles automatically.

Legitimate references, useful partnerships and authentic reviews support trust/referrals; DA/DR is not Google PageRank. Reject manipulative paid links/PBNs/doorway pages, mass directories, fake reviews and hidden model instructions. Contact/spend/public replies require actual mandate. Diagnose suspicious backlinks before recommending disavow or broad cleanup.
