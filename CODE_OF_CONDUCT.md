# Code of Conduct

Treat contributors with respect. Discuss evidence and behavior rather than personal characteristics. Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, deliberate privacy violations, and publication of others' private information are unacceptable.

Give constructive, specific feedback and disclose uncertainty. Do not misrepresent authorship, credentials, review independence, provider participation, or test results. Keep security reports and private deployment information in the appropriate private channel.

The initial moderator is [MFSCV](https://github.com/MFSCV). Use an established private channel to report misconduct, or open a non-sensitive issue requesting private contact without posting personal information. Security matters follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). No staffed response-time commitment or independent appeals body currently exists; requests for reconsideration may be made privately to the maintainer.

The maintainer may request corrections, remove harmful content, temporarily restrict participation, or exclude repeated offenders. Decisions should be proportionate, respect privacy as far as practical, and disclose conflicts of interest. These rules apply to repository issues, pull requests, reviews, and other project-managed spaces.
